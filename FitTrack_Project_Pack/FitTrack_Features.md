# FitTrack — Product Requirements

## Product definition
An all-age adult fitness management platform for users aged 18–60+, designed around Indian food habits and multiple training disciplines: calisthenics, gym, weightlifting, Taekwondo, cardio, mobility and recovery.

## Target users
- Adults 18–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50–59 and 60+.
- Beginners through advanced users.
- Home, gym and martial-arts-school training.
- Vegetarian, eggetarian, non-vegetarian and vegan food preferences.
- Users seeking strength, muscle, general fitness, endurance, mobility or weight-management support.

## MVP must-have
1. Authentication and profile onboarding.
2. Fitness goals and training preferences.
3. Dashboard.
4. Exercise library.
5. Workout planner and workout logging.
6. Calisthenics, gym and weightlifting logs.
7. Basic progress and personal records.
8. Responsive UI and secure user-owned data.

## Release 2
- Indian food catalogue and meal diary.
- Nutrition and hydration tracking.
- Taekwondo training log.
- Mobility, cardio and recovery logs.
- Weekly/monthly reports and export.

## Release 3
- AI fitness assistant.
- Editable AI-generated training templates.
- Wearable / Health Connect integration.
- Coach portal with explicit consent.
- Community and challenges.

## Key acceptance criteria
- Users can register, log in and securely access only their own records.
- Users can create, edit and complete a training session.
- A session can include multiple exercise types and record sets/reps/load/time/distance.
- Exercise search can filter by training mode, equipment and difficulty.
- Users can log Indian meals with portion size and see estimated nutrition where data exists.
- Users can log Taekwondo practice separately from gym or calisthenics sessions.
- Users can view progress over time and export their records.
- Every screen supports loading, empty, error and success states.
- Private photos and health-related records are protected by access controls.
- App clearly distinguishes user-entered data, estimates and AI-generated suggestions.

## Safety and trust
- Age is one personalisation input, not a standalone basis for prescribing exercise.
- No diagnosis, treatment claims or guaranteed results.
- Medical conditions, injury, pregnancy, recent surgery or concerning symptoms should trigger professional-care guidance.
- Qualified supervision for complex lifting and Taekwondo sparring/advanced techniques.
- Nutrition figures are estimates unless verified; no restrictive or therapeutic diet prescriptions.
- Private-by-default data, clear consent, export and deletion pathways.
